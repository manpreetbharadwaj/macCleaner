Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026 · Draft — to be reviewed before publishing.

The short version

MacBook Cleaner runs entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, upload or sell information about you or your files.

What the app reads

To show what’s using your storage, the app reads file and folder names, sizes and dates in locations such as Downloads, Desktop, Documents, Movies, your Library caches and your Applications folder. File contents are only read to confirm that two files are exact duplicates, and that check happens locally.

What the app stores

Your settings, exclusions, schedule and cleanup history are saved in a file on your Mac (~/Library/Application Support/com.macbookcleaner.app). Diagnostic logs are kept in ~/Library/Logs/com.macbookcleaner.app. Neither is sent anywhere.

Network access

The app does not send data over the network. Links such as “Check for Updates” simply open this website in your browser.

Permissions

macOS may ask you to allow access to certain folders, and you can optionally grant Full Disk Access. You can revoke either at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us through this website.
